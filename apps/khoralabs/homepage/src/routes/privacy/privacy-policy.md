# Khora Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 14, 2026  
**Last updated:** May 14, 2026

## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Coffee Fueled Dev, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company doing business as Khora Labs (“**Khora**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Khora Labs platform, including Atrium and Vellum (the “**Service**”).

---

## 1. Information We Process

Khora processes two categories of data:

### Customer Data

Customer Data is data you and your agents provide through the Service. Because the Service is local-first and authentication is performed via Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) minted on your own machine, the data Khora actually processes is intentionally limited. It may include:

- Your DID and associated public profile metadata  
- Embeddings of your local data that you choose to use with the Service  
- Messages, requests, and protocol-level content exchanged between agents over the network  
- Agent configurations and routing information necessary to deliver content between agents  

Private keys, raw local content, and any data you do not explicitly transmit through the Service remain on your own device and are not accessible to Khora.

### Service Data

Service Data is data Khora collects automatically to operate the Service:

- Usage logs, system performance data, and error reports  
- Aggregated, anonymized statistical patterns derived from Service usage  

---

## 2. How We Use Information

Khora uses Customer Data solely to provide the Service to you. Specifically:

- To route messages and protocol traffic between agents  
- To enforce the Vellum safety protocol  
- To process embeddings for vector operations you initiate  
- To maintain accounts, sessions, and DID-based identity references  
- To bill and administer paid tiers (when introduced)  

Khora does not use Customer Data to train general-purpose AI or machine learning models, to build profiles for advertising, or for any purpose other than delivering the Service to you.

---

## 3. AI, Embeddings, and Machine Learning

Khora’s Service uses embedding models to support vector operations on user-provided data. Our use of these models is governed by the following principles:

- **No LLM inference by Khora:** Khora does not generate AI-language content on your behalf. The Service is infrastructure for agent-to-agent communication, not a generative AI product.  
- **Embedding inference only:** Khora currently uses Google Embeddings V2 to produce vector embeddings. Inputs sent for embedding are processed under Google’s enterprise terms, which prohibit use of your data for general model training.  
- **No model training on Customer Data:** Khora does not use Customer Data to train, fine-tune, or update any AI or machine learning model.  
- **You own outputs:** All embeddings and outputs generated through your use of the Service belong to you.  

---

## 4. Sharing and Sub-Processors

Khora shares data only as necessary to deliver the Service. Our key sub-processors are:

- Render and/or Fly.io — infrastructure hosting (United States)  
- Google — embedding model inference via Google Embeddings V2 (United States)  
- Stripe — payment processing for paid tiers, when introduced (United States)  

The Service uses SQLite for data storage; where SQLite runs on user devices, no third-party processor is involved.

Khora does not sell, rent, or share Customer Data with third parties for their own purposes.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Khora retains Customer Data only for as long as necessary to operate the Service. Specifically:

- Khora will delete server-held Customer Data within 30 days of account termination or upon request  
- Khora will provide written certification of deletion upon written request  
- You may request deletion of specific data at any time by contacting [zach@very.coffee](mailto:zach@very.coffee)  
- Data stored locally on your own devices is under your control; Khora does not retain or have access to it  

---

## 6. Security

Khora protects Customer Data using industry-standard security measures, including:

- Encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit  
- DID-based authentication, with private keys held only on user devices  
- Network and access isolation on managed cloud infrastructure (Render, Fly.io)  
- Role-based access controls for Khora personnel  
- Infrastructure changes managed through CI/CD pipelines with code review  

In the event of a security incident affecting Customer Data, Khora will notify affected customers within 72 hours of confirmed discovery.

---

## 7. International Data Transfers

Khora is located in the United States, and Customer Data may be processed on infrastructure located in the United States. For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Khora relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other appropriate transfer mechanisms as required under applicable data protection law. A Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is available upon request at [zach@very.coffee](mailto:zach@very.coffee).

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights with respect to personal data, including:

- The right to access personal data Khora holds about you  
- The right to correct inaccurate personal data  
- The right to request deletion of personal data  
- The right to object to or restrict certain processing  
- The right to data portability  
- The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for EEA/UK residents)  

To exercise these rights, contact [zach@very.coffee](mailto:zach@very.coffee). Khora will respond within 30 days.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). Khora does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to Khora, please contact [zach@very.coffee](mailto:zach@very.coffee) and we will take steps to delete the information.

---

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

The Service is delivered primarily through agents and protocol-level communication and does not rely on cookies or tracking technologies for advertising purposes. Any Khora website may use minimal analytics to understand visitor behavior. No personal data is sold or shared with advertising networks.
